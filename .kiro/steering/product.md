# Vera Protocol - Product Vision

## Core Identity
Vera is an AI-mediated freelance escrow platform that acts as a **neutral Sentinel** between freelancers and clients. The platform eliminates payment disputes through intelligent milestone management and automated arbitration.

## Voice-to-Text Guardian
- Vera operates as a voice-enabled guardian, making the platform accessible to non-technical users
- Designers, writers, and other creatives can interact naturally without coding knowledge
- Voice commands handle project setup, milestone tracking, and dispute resolution

## Neutrality Principle
The AI Sentinel maintains strict neutrality by:
- **Objective Milestone Assessment**: 90% of project value tied to measurable technical deliverables
- **Subjective Variance Buffer**: 10% held for style/preference disputes
- **Evidence-Based Decisions**: All arbitration based on documented requirements and deliverables
- **No Bias Toward Either Party**: Equal treatment of freelancer and client concerns

## Mass Market Accessibility
- **Zero-Code Interface**: Non-developers can create and manage projects through intuitive UI
- **Natural Language Processing**: Plain English project descriptions automatically converted to technical milestones
- **Visual Progress Tracking**: Clear, non-technical status updates for all stakeholders
- **Voice-First Design**: Primary interaction through speech, with visual backup

## Success Metrics
- Dispute resolution time under 24 hours
- 95%+ automatic milestone releases without human intervention
- Accessibility score of AA+ for non-technical users
- Voice command accuracy above 98%

## Target Users
- **Primary**: Non-technical clients (small business owners, entrepreneurs)
- **Secondary**: Creative freelancers (designers, writers, marketers)
- **Tertiary**: Technical freelancers seeking dispute-free payments
